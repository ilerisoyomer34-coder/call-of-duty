## English

**App name:** `Demir Şafak`

**Subtitle (App Store):** `Tactical Squad Shooter`

**Short description (Google Play):** `Lead Alfa Team, complete missions, rank up. A tactical squad shooter.`

**Keywords (App Store):** `fps,shooter,war,soldier,tactical,squad,sniper,commando,military,action,offline`

**Full description:**

```
Hit the front line before dawn. Demir Şafak ("Iron Dawn") is a first-person tactical shooter where you command Alfa Team.

LEAD YOUR SQUAD
• Alfa-1 Demir, Alfa-2 Kaya (medic) and Alfa-3 Yıldız (machine gunner) fight at your side.
• Give orders with one tap on the command wheel: follow, hold position, suppress, take cover, clear area, heal me.
• Ping a target and your squad moves on it. Typed and voice commands in Turkish.
• Go down and your squad runs to revive you; revive them in return.

HOLD THE LINE
• 6 missions across 5 maps: Red Sands, Harbour, Ruined City, Snowy Pass, Night Refinery.
• Heavy machine-gun nests, anti-aircraft guns and tanks; every mission ends with a helicopter extraction.
• Three difficulty levels and bonus objectives in every mission.

BUILD YOUR LOADOUT
• 18 weapons and a combat knife: assault rifles, machine guns, sniper rifles, shotguns, a bazooka and the special minigun.
• Every weapon has its own sight: red dot, holographic, ACOG and distinct scopes.
• Body armour, helmets, grenades, smoke and first aid.

RANK UP
• Earn XP from missions: 15 ranks and 100 levels.
• Three daily challenges and a 7-day login reward.

ONLINE
• Team up with friends for Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch and Co-op against AI.
• Online play opens with a server link shared by a friend.

• No ads. No real-money purchases. The campaign plays offline.
• Touch controls; keyboard and mouse supported.
```

## İnceleme notu (App Review / Play inceleme), İngilizce

```
Demir Şafak is a complete single-player game bundled in the app; the campaign works fully offline (no login).
Online play is optional and community-hosted: a player joins a friend's server by pasting an invite link
(Online screen). Online accounts are created with a player name only; they can be deleted in
Settings → Online account → Delete account. Players can report and block others with the "⋯" button
on any player row; reports reach the server owner, who removes offending accounts (server/moderate.mjs).
No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. All weapon names in the store build are fictional.
```
